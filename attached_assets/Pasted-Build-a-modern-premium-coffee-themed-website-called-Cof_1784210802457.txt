========================================================
AI BARISTA
========================================================

The website has a permanent character.

A cute coffee mug.

The mug represents the AI Barista.

The mug stays fixed in the bottom-right corner on every page.

It is NOT just a chat button.

It is a character.

Design:

Cute smiling coffee mug.

Minimal face.

Small eyes.

Friendly smile.

Steam coming from the mug.

Idle animations:

Steam gently moves.

Eyes blink occasionally.

Tiny floating movement.

Very subtle.

Every 30-60 seconds display a small speech bubble.

Examples:

"Need a recommendation?"

"Looking for something sweet?"

"Fresh beans make all the difference."

"Still deciding?"

Bubble disappears automatically.

When the mug is clicked:

The mug performs a tiny bounce animation.

A beautiful right-side panel slides into view.

The page behind remains visible.

The chat panel contains

Header:

☕ The Barista

Welcome message:

"Hello! What can I brew for you today?"

Chat history area.

Input box.

Send button.

The chatbot functionality can be placeholder for now.

Only create the UI.
