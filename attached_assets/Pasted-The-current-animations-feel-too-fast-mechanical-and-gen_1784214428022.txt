The current animations feel too fast, mechanical, and generic.

Think Apple, Aesop, Linear, Arc Browser, and Framer—not a typical React template.

ANIMATION PHILOSOPHY

The website should feel calm.

Nothing should move suddenly.

Nothing should bounce aggressively.

Every animation should feel intentional.
