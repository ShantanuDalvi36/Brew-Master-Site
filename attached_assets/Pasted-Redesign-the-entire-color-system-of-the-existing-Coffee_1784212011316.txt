Redesign the entire color system of the existing Coffee Diaries website while preserving the current layout, components, spacing, and animations.

The objective is to make the website feel like a premium coffee journal with a calm, elegant, matte finish.

DO NOT redesign the UI structure.
DO NOT change the navigation or page layout.
Only improve the visual identity through a professional color system.

==================================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

The website should feel like:

- A luxury specialty coffee café
- A leather-bound coffee journal
- Warm and inviting
- Minimal and elegant
- Soft matte finish
- Editorial and timeless

Avoid bright colors.

Avoid high saturation.

Avoid glossy effects.

Everything should feel warm and comfortable.
